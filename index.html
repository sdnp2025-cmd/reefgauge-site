On the roadmap
What it shows

Eight gauges, one look.

The tank card

Temperature, pH, salinity, alkalinity, calcium, magnesium, nitrate and phosphate as ring gauges, each with its target range and a 24-hour trend line underneath. Green when it's right, amber when it's drifting, red when it isn't.

Alerts you can't walk past

When a parameter leaves its range, a pop-up takes over the screen and the panel's speakers sound — at any hour for the urgent ones, quiet hours respected for the rest. Tap Got it and it retreats to the ticker along the bottom until it's fixed. Pushes to your phone too.

Chemistry that means something

Your ICP results on dials, with the elements that are out of range in the colours you'd expect. Log water changes and dosing; ReefGauge turns a low reading into a measured dose for the supplements you actually use — two-part, Red Sea, Triton, Balling, ESV, Moonshiners.

The room, and the weather

A CO₂ sensor in the display watches the air the tank breathes — high room CO₂ pulls pH down — alongside temperature and humidity. Outside, the local forecast and radar, because a storm on the way is a power cut on the way.

Questions

The short answers.

Do I need an Apex?

No, but it's the best case. With an Apex and Trident everything is automatic. Without a controller you still get ICP results, the chemistry log and dosing, room air, weather and alerts on what you log by hand.

Does it need the internet?

Only for the weather, radar and phone pushes. Your controller, your ReefBeat gear and the display talk over your own network; if the internet goes down the reef screen keeps working.

Is there a subscription?

No. You buy the display, it's yours. Software updates are free and install from the settings screen.

Can I see it from my phone?

Not yet — remote viewing is the first thing after launch. For now you get alerts on your phone, and the display itself in the room.

What about my other gear?

HYDROS and GHL are next. If you run something else, tell us what — the list of what people own decides what comes after.

When, and how much?

Early 2027. Price to be announced; the launch list hears first, and hears about the introductory price.
